The Agrima 5-Point Hygiene Pledge

Your skin and health are sacred. We follow strict sterilization standards for all in-studio sessions.

🧴

UV & Dry Heat Sterilization

Every metal tweezer, scissor, and cuticle pusher is sterilized in medical UV dry heat cabinets between every single client.

🪄

Single-Use Applicators

Zero double-dipping. We strictly utilize disposable mascara wands, lip gloss wands, and biodegradable facial wipes.

💎

100% Genuine Certified Brands

Only certified cosmetics: MAC, Huda Beauty, Temptu Airbrush, Kryolan, and L'Oreal Professional. No duplicates ever.

🫧

Deep Sanitized Brushes

Makeup brushes are deep-cleansed with antimicrobial washes and 99% alcohol sanitizers before touching any face.
