# 🌸 Agrima Makeup Store

Welcome to the official web application for **Agrima Makeup Store**, a premium in-studio beauty parlour and bridal makeup salon located in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. 

Designed with modern aesthetics, warm luxury styling, and intuitive booking interactions, this platform offers clients a seamless browsing experience across all beauty, skincare, hair, and bridal packages with direct WhatsApp appointment scheduling.

---

## ✨ Features of the Project

- **Strict 100% In-Studio Experience**
  - Highlighting client-first salon benefits: shadowless vanity mirror lighting, climate-controlled AC studio environment, and medical-grade sanitized equipment.
  - Transparent policy notice ensuring quality, comfort, and professional standards.

- **Curated 3-Tier Pricing Packages**
  - **🟢 Budget-Friendly Daily Care**: Quick threading, cleanups, basic facials, and budget party styling.
  - **⭐ Premium Glamour**: Premium facials, HD party makeup, and engagement packages.
  - **👑 Luxury Bridal Suites**: Basic Bridal, HD Bridal, and Airbrush Bridal packages with jewelry and dupatta setting.

- **Departmental Service Catalog**
  - Interactive, categorized menus covering **Threading & Waxing**, **Facial & Skincare**, **Hair Styling & Care**, and **Mehndi (Henna) Art**.

- **Interactive WhatsApp Multi-Service Cart**
  - Add multiple treatments or packages to a dynamic slide-out booking cart.
  - Live cost calculation in INR (₹).
  - Client enters their contact information, appointment date, and preferred time slot.
  - One-click checkout creates a pre-formatted, organized WhatsApp booking message ready to send.

- **Instant 1-Click Quick Booking**
  - Every service card features a dedicated WhatsApp reservation button for rapid booking.

- **Interactive Transformation Showcase**
  - Side-by-side bridal before/after comparison slider showcasing real salon transformations.

- **FAQ & Studio Guarantee**
  - Answers to frequent client questions regarding walk-ins, bridal booking timelines, hygiene protocols, and payment options.

- **Mobile-First & Responsive Design**
  - Fluid, high-speed experience optimized across mobile phones, tablets, and desktops.
  - Fast-action floating call and WhatsApp buttons for instant mobile contact.
